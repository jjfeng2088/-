Tap a key to set the pitch; hold and slide across keys to hear a glide. Computer keys A W S E D F T G Y H U J K play C4–C5, and the space bar starts and stops the sound.

Pitch smoothing

Pitch changes pass through a second-order low-pass filter. A higher Q gives more overshoot, which sounds closer to how real singers move between notes.

F1 / F2 vowel map

Tap or drag on the map to set F1 and F2 together. Reference points are typical adult male vowel values.

An independent reimplementation of the features of Madde, the Windows synthesizer by Svante Granqvist (Tolvan Data); not affiliated with the original author. It uses additive synthesis: each harmonic's amplitude comes from the source tilt and the resonance curves of six formants. If an iPhone plays no sound, turn off the silent switch on its side.
